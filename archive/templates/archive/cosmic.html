Did You Know?

A few quick facts from our cosmic journey.

Our Sun

The Sun is the star at the center of our Solar System and provides the energy that makes life on Earth possible.

🪐

Gas Giants

Jupiter and Saturn are enormous gas giants, while Uranus and Neptune are classified as ice giants.

🌍

Our Home

Earth is currently the only world known to support life and contains large amounts of liquid surface water.
